What is Plinko game and how the board works

What is Plinko game? It is a pegboard betting game where the ball falls through staggered pegs and ends in one of several pockets. Every bounce is random, so players cannot force a result. The board is shaped like a triangle, and each row makes the path more uncertain as the ball moves downward.

That setup is why Plinko gambling is easy to follow but still has variance. The middle pockets are usually more common, while the side pockets are harder to hit and may pay more.
